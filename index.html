My approach

Risk management is the whole game.

Anyone can find a winning trade. Staying in the game long enough to compound is what separates funded traders from blown accounts. Here's the framework I run on.

Capital protection

I risk a small, fixed fraction of the account per idea and cap daily exposure well inside prop-firm drawdown limits. Survival is non-negotiable.

Defined edge

Every entry is tied to clear technical structure and a favourable risk-to-reward profile. If it doesn't meet the criteria, I don't take it.

Patience & timing

I wait for high-probability setups instead of forcing trades. Sitting on my hands is a position too — and often the most profitable one.

Trading psychology

Emotion is the real account-killer. I run a strict routine, detach from individual outcomes, and trade the process — not the P&L.

Journaling & review

Every trade is logged, tagged and reviewed. Data — not feelings — tells me what's working and what to cut.

Consistency

Same rules, same risk, same routine — across winning and losing streaks. Repeatability is what makes results fundable.
